Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 28, 2026

Phantom Note ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website at phantomnote.art. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how we handle it.

1. Data We Collect

No personal data is required to use Phantom Note. All music processing (scale visualization, chord detection, pitch analysis, lyrics scrolling) happens entirely in your browser. We do not receive or store your audio, MIDI, or lyrics content.

We collect the following limited data:

  • Analytics (Plausible): We use Plausible Analytics, a privacy-first, cookie-free analytics tool. Plausible collects aggregated, anonymized data (page views, referrers, browser type, country). It does not use cookies and does not track individuals across sites. See plausible.io/privacy.
  • Advertising (Google AdSense): We display ads via Google AdSense. Google may use cookies and device identifiers to show personalized ads based on your browsing history. You can opt out at adssettings.google.com. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Local Storage: Saved songs are stored locally in your browser using localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

2. Cookies

Phantom Note itself does not set cookies. Google AdSense may set third-party advertising cookies. Plausible Analytics is cookie-free.

3. Microphone Access

The Pitch Detection and Practice Mode features request access to your microphone via the browser's standard permission system. Audio is processed entirely in your browser in real time and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted to our servers.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Plausible Analytics — privacy-first page analytics
  • Google AdSense — advertising
  • Google Fonts — typography (font files served by Google)
  • Tone.js — audio library loaded from Cloudflare CDN

5. Children's Privacy

Phantom Note is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the service evolves (e.g., when subscriptions are added). The "Last updated" date above will reflect any changes.

7. Contact

Questions? Email us at hello@phantomnote.art.
